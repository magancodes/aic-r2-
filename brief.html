A factory that looks fine until it isn't — and a twin that sees it first.

A sensor-poor assembly-line digital twin: a synthetic plant that hides its own physics, an estimator allowed to see only events and layout, and a referee that is the one component permitted to grade them against the truth. Built and measured over seven days.

40 stations · 12 dark seed 20260822 0 runtime dependencies ~4,400 lines reproducible ✓
The one-paragraph version

Real plants are under-instrumented: a wear fault can build scrap for the better part of an hour before anyone sees it. This project builds the whole loop end-to-end — a believable factory, an estimator kept deliberately blind to the factory's hidden state, and a scoring harness — then answers four operational questions with measured, reproducible numbers: where is the bottleneck, is it about to move, which welds are going bad, and what can't we see at all. Every figure on this page is emitted by the test suite into results.json.

01 · The brief

What the project had to prove.

There is no separate spec sheet — the requirements are the design contract the build was held to, and they are enforced in code (an isolation gate) and in a scoring harness. Reconstructed plainly:

  • R1 · realism
    A believable factory. A synthetic assembly line whose behaviour — cycle-time variation, electrode wear, lot-to-lot fit-up, random faults, in-line inspection — is rich enough that estimating it is a real problem, and reproducible bit-for-bit from a seed.
  • R2 · isolation
    The twin stays blind. The estimator may consume only the event stream and the line layout — never the plant's hidden physics or ground truth. This is the central rule; it is enforced automatically before every run.
  • R3 · sensor-poor
    Work under real gaps. 12 of 40 stations emit nothing at all ("dark"), and there is no in-process weld-quality sensor — the twin must reason through the gaps, not around them.
  • R4 · decisions
    Answer operational questions, each with a falsifiable exit test: locate the bottleneck, forecast it before it moves, catch welds going bad in-process, and put calibrated numbers on the stations nobody can see.
  • R5 · honesty
    Numbers over narrative. Every quoted figure must come from the harness output, baselines must be beaten on their own terms, and metrics must not flatter the result.
02 · Architecture

Three programs, one contract.

The plant knows everything and emits little. The twin sees only what leaves the floor. The harness is the sole component allowed to look at both sides — so any score is honest by construction.

Day 4

Forecast the bottleneck before it bites

Goal

Flag a developing slowdown before it shows up in throughput.

Method

Monte-Carlo the line forward from a Normal-Gamma posterior-predictive belief; inject +80% slowdown at S25 and measure the lead time.

Day 5

Catch electrode wear in-process

Goal

Detect bad welds from the dynamic-resistance (DR) curve, and beat the SPC control-chart baseline.

Method

From-scratch Isolation Forest + autoencoder + SPC on 12 geometric DR features. Reported honestly under heavy class imbalance (12 scrap welds in ~17.5k).

92% of alerts are genuine wear · all scrap caught 8.4 min early · SPC caught 0/12beats SPC ✓
Day 6

See the dark stations

Goal

Put calibrated numbers on the 12 stations that emit nothing, and rank where a new sensor would pay off most.

Method

Virtual metrology from instrumented neighbours with 80% credible bands; Value-of-Information ranking for sensor placement.

80% band covered truth 79.4% of the time (n=200) · VoI ranks S20/S21 nextwell-calibrated ✓
Day 7

Prove the recommendation is causal

Goal

Test Roser's falsifiable claim: if APM says S22 is the bottleneck X% of the time, a 1 s improvement there should move takt within a predicted band.

Method

Predict the ΔIAT band from shifting-% on the stock line, then causally probe the plant and check the measured gain lands inside it.

05 · The honesty & rigor pass

Where we made the story truer.

A review pass hardened the reporting without touching the plant physics or the twin's models — the improvements are all in how results are measured and shown.

Day 5 detector: an honest headline

Before — flattering

7% precision

ROC-AUC 0.987 and "FAR 0.59%" hid the real per-weld precision. Under a 12-in-17,500 class imbalance, both numbers look great while the operating point is poor.

After — honest

92% real alerts

Average Precision reported alongside AUC, and every alert classified: 8 confirmed scrap + 94 pre-scrap early warnings on the known worn electrode, and only 9 genuine false alarms in 13,140 normal welds.

The 103 apparent "false positives" were the electrode's own degradation ramp — real early warnings, not errors. A precision–recall sweep now exposes the trade-off, and a recommended operating point catches every scrap weld 8.4 min before the first defect.

Forecast, tightened

Replaced a nested per-slice simulation and a guard-loop hack with one clean rollout per sample using line callbacks — identical lead time (median ~12 min), far clearer code.

Isolation gate, hardened

The blindness check now scans code with comments stripped, reports file:line and a reason per violation, and the dead no-op branch is gone. Verified with positive and negative tests.

Paper-scale evidence

Re-ran the full suite at n=200 for tighter confidence intervals: Day-4 p90 tightened 18.5 → 14.6 min; Day-6 coverage 79.4% — well-calibrated at the larger sample.

What deploys to Vercel

The static front-end — dashboard + interactive console — is browser-only and Vercel-ready (vercel.json, no build step, cleanUrls). Import the repo, framework "Other", deploy — or merge to main and the Git integration auto-deploys.

What does not run on Vercel

The engine (plant/ twin/ harness/) is Node scripts, not a web service. They produce results.json locally or in CI; the site just displays those numbers.

08 · For reviewers

What to look at, and what's honest to flag.

Strongest evidence

  • The isolation contract is enforced in code, not just claimed
  • Every headline number traces to results.json from a fixed seed
  • Baselines are beaten on their own terms (APM > utilisation; detector > SPC)
  • Methods that can't separate are reported as such (Day 3, 1.44σ)

Stated limitations

  • Scrap is rare (12 welds) — precision–recall is the honest lens, not raw FAR
  • The isolation gate is a code-level guard, not a sandbox
  • The plant is synthetic; numbers are internally valid, not yet field-validated
  • Day 8 rehearsal and broader-seed sweeps are the natural next step
