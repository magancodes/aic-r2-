Week 1

Monte Carlo: run the next 30 minutes, 300 times.

From the line's current state, the twin rolls the dice on every cycle time and plays the future out hundreds of ways. Where most futures agree, that's the forecast: "Station 3 crosses the bottleneck threshold in ~22 minutes, 78% confidence." Bottlenecks are ranked with the active-period method, because utilization lies.

threshold crossed · ~22 min · 78%
Week 2

Every weld and every bolt gets checked, not sampled.

A car body carries 5,000 to 8,000 spot welds, and today most plants check them with random sampling after the body is done. We score every single one, in line, from its dynamic resistance curve, and every bolt from its torque angle curve. Published models hit 97% accuracy this way. The detectors train only on normal operation, because real plants have no labeled defects. An Isolation Forest filters every sample in under a millisecond, an autoencoder takes a second look at anything odd, and every alert is graded against QC results later, so false alarms die out instead of piling up. This drift hides inside normal control limits, which is exactly where SPC charts stay quiet.

Ongoing

Anyone can ask the line a question.

The copilot is tool-calling only: it translates the question into a simulation run and reads the result back. It never answers from its own imagination, and that is exactly why a plant can trust it.
