DigitalTwin.ai · architecture

Four moves, one mirror.

Data leaves the floor as events, becomes a living simulation, and comes back as a decision. Nothing else in between.

01 · SENSE

Clip on, don't cut in

event: part_done S2 · 4.1s

Current clamps and part counters at ₹6–8k per station, or one camera watching four. No PLC touched. Every station becomes an event stream.

02 · MIRROR

A ghost of the line

TWIN ↑ · FLOOR ↓ · SYNCED EVERY EVENT

A discrete event flow twin boots from the line's standard times as Bayesian priors, then live events keep correcting it. Bottlenecks are found with the active period method, because utilization lies: a station can run at 95% and still not be your constraint.

03 · PREDICT

Run the future, 300×

crosses threshold · ~22 min

Monte Carlo rollouts forecast the next constraint 20 to 30 minutes out. For defects, every weld's dynamic resistance curve and every bolt's torque angle curve is scored in line. Published models predict weld quality at 97% accuracy before the body is even complete. An Isolation Forest plus autoencoder pair catches drift that hides inside normal control limits, and every alert is graded against QC results so false alarms die out.

04 · ASK

Talk to the line

Twin: throughput −9%, S3 stays the constraint. Safe to proceed. (300 rollouts, 1.8 s)

An LLM copilot, tool-calling only. It sets up the run, the simulation gives the answer. The LLM is the interface; the simulation is the truth.
